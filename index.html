How the atlas works

I

Learn

Map the world before you sail it.

Start on the foundation islands: what AI really is, Python, the math and how models learn. Short, honest explanations and the best free courses, picked and ordered for you.

  • Six foundation islands shared by nearly every path
  • Free courses first, paid only when clearly better
  • A checklist of ideas to explain out loud before moving on
II

Build

Every age has its inventions. Build this one's.

Each island ends with a real project: a script, a chatbot over your PDFs, a fine-tuned model, a GPT written from an empty file. You learn by making the thing.

  • One project per island, sized for a weekend or two
  • Live demos you can play with right on the page
  • Projects stack into a portfolio
III

Grow

Grow alongside the machines.

Choose a path for where you want to go: build apps on models, train your own, build one from scratch, or go deep in vision, language, generative media, robotics-style RL, MLOps or safety.

  • Ten paths with honest trade-offs and real job data
  • Track your progress island by island
  • Your pace: 5, 10 or 20 hours a week
