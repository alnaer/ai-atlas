How does byte-pair encoding build a vocabulary?

It starts with single bytes or characters as the tokens. It then finds the pair of neighboring tokens that occurs most often in the training text, merges it into a new token, and repeats until the vocabulary reaches the size you chose. Common words end up as one token and rare words as several pieces.

Why do filtering and deduplicating data matter so much in pretraining?

A model learns whatever its data contains. Spam, boilerplate and broken text teach it bad habits, and many copies of the same text waste compute and push the model to memorize instead of generalize. Cleaning the data often improves a model more than changing its architecture.

What do mixed precision and multi-GPU training speed up, and what do they cost?

Mixed precision runs most of the math in 16-bit instead of 32-bit numbers, which cuts memory use and speeds up training, at the price of extra care with numerical stability. Multi-GPU training splits the work across GPUs to finish sooner, but adds communication overhead and complexity, so doubling the GPUs gives less than double the speed.

What do scaling laws say?

That a language model's loss falls in a smooth, predictable way as you increase model size, data and compute. This lets you forecast the result of a large run from small ones, and work out how to split a fixed compute budget between a bigger model and more training data.
